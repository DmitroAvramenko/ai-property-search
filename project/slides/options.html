Integration approaches

Two ways to bring AI into search

Option 1

AI inside existing filters

Natural-language field in the filters panel

AI populates the matching filters

Users review and adjust criteria manually

Lowest effort: extends the current PoC

Option 2

Dedicated AI search experience

AI search field as the primary entry point

AI processes the request, opens results

Classic filters remain for refinement

Most intuitive, AI becomes central

Not mutually exclusive: Option 1 can ship first and become the engine behind Option 2.
